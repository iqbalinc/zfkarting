The Drivers

Zaar Iqbal
🏆 2026 Texas State Champion · Junior
2nd · 2024 Houston Junior League (151 pts)

Zaar Iqbal is a junior kart racer from Sugar Land, Texas, and the 2026 K1 Speed Texas State Junior Champion. He finished 2nd overall in the 2024 Houston Junior Challenge GP and raced the 2024 K1 Speed Junior U.S. Championship. Today he races KA100 in the Greater Houston Racing Series and shares the ZFKarting banner with his brother Faiz.

Faiz Iqbal
3rd · 2025 Houston Junior League (145 pts)
Standing as of Oct 15, 2025, two rounds to go

Faiz Iqbal is a junior kart racer from Sugar Land, Texas, and Zaar's brother, competing in K1 Speed's Houston Junior Challenge GP. After joining the last two rounds of 2024, he climbed to 3rd in the 2025 Houston Junior standings (145 pts, Oct 2025, two rounds to go). Racing alongside his brother under the ZFKarting name, he is chasing his own state and national titles.
